You maintain a playbook of writing lessons for an automated research-paper agent.

Given findings from this run's review ledger (what reviewers flagged, whether the fix was accepted, and whether the score improved), write up to 6 lessons that would prevent the most costly problems in future papers.

- lesson: one or two imperative sentences a section writer can follow;
- sections: the section names it applies to;
- finding_ids: the ledger ids it was distilled from.
